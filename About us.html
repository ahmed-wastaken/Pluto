We replace complexity and recurring costs with solutions built around what businesses actually need. This results in less wasted time and lower costs.

The Origin

Why We Started Pluto

The technology industry changed its priorities over time. Software transitioned from being purchased and owned to per-user subscriptions. Building a professional website required large upfront investments and ongoing fees. Marketing agencies relied on retainers and restrictive contracts, measuring success through impressions and clicks instead of actual business growth.

We created Pluto to rethink how technology and business services are delivered. Companies should not overpay for software or commit to expensive contracts without accountability. They also should not spend hours on tasks that can be automated. We build solutions that make financial sense and deliver measurable value.
