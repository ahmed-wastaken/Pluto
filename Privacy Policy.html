This policy describes our privacy practices, what Personal Information we collect, what we do with it, how we share it, and your rights regarding that information. This covers our main website as well as the suite of digital services we provide, including Custom Software, eCommerce, B2B Lead Generation, and our Education Suite.

In Plain English: We believe in being transparent about your data. This page explains exactly what we collect, how we keep it safe, and the rights you have over your own information.

1. What "Personal Information" Do We Collect?

To provide our services, we collect information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

  • Information you provide us: When you fill out forms on our site, request a quote, or contact our support team, we collect your name, email, phone number, and project details.
  • Information we collect automatically: When you use our website, we collect aggregated usage data. This includes your IP address, browser type, and session interactions. To optimize user experience and troubleshoot issues, we use session-replay technology that logs keystrokes and data entered into our "Contact Us" form, even if the form is not submitted.
  • Information from other sources: We may receive information about you from third-party sources, such as public B2B databases, LinkedIn, and lead enhancement partners, to build targeted outreach lists for our clients.

In Plain English: We collect the info you give us directly, technical data about how you browse our site (including what you type into our contact forms), and professional data from public business directories.

2. Why Do We Collect Your Information?

We use your Personal Information for the following purposes:

  • To operate and manage our services, including delivering custom software, eCommerce platforms, and Google Workspace environments.
  • To conduct B2B lead generation and targeted cold-email outreach on behalf of our clients.
  • To analyze website traffic, improve our user interface, and recover abandoned inquiries.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and protect against fraud.

In Plain English: We use your data to make our services work, connect businesses with the right leads, improve our website, and keep everything secure.

3. Service-Specific Data (Education & B2B)

Because we offer highly specialized tools, we process specific types of sensitive data strictly on behalf of our clients (acting as a "Data Processor" or "Service Provider"):

  • Education Suite (Schools & Students): We process student attendance via AI facial recognition technology and Wi-Fi verified QR codes. We also process student health records (clinic visit reports), academic transcripts, and tuition payment histories. Note: Pluto only processes this data under the strict direction of the educational institution. Schools are responsible for obtaining parental consent under COPPA and FERPA.
  • B2B Lead Generation: We scrape, verify, and compile professional contact information to launch personalized email campaigns. We manage domains and outreach infrastructure to connect our clients with verified prospects.

In Plain English: If your school uses our Education Suite, we process student data (like facial recognition for attendance or clinic notes) only because the school asked us to, and they are in charge of getting parent permission. For our B2B clients, we gather public professional contact info to help them find new business.

4. How Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with trusted third parties to run our business:

  • Service Providers: We share data with hosting providers, analytics services (like Google Analytics), and payment processors (like Stripe or Avalara for tax calculation).
  • Legal & Law Enforcement: We will disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes.

In Plain English: We don't sell your data to shady data brokers. We only share it with the secure tech partners we use to run our business (like our payment processors or cloud hosts), or if the law absolutely forces us to.

5. Your US Privacy Rights (CCPA & State Laws)

If you reside in California or other states with comprehensive privacy laws, you have specific rights regarding your data:

  • The Right to Know: You can ask us what specific personal data we have collected about you.
  • The Right to Delete/Correct: You can request that we delete or correct your personal information.
  • The Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of our B2B marketing communications or cold email outreach at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" or contacting us.

In Plain English: Depending on where you live, you have the legal right to ask us what data we have on you, tell us to fix it, or demand that we delete it. You can always opt out of our marketing emails.

6. Security and Retention

We maintain robust, ISO 27001-compliant security controls, including 24/7 monitoring, DDoS protection, and SSL encryption. We retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by statutory retention obligations.

In Plain English: We lock down your data using top-tier, enterprise-grade security. We only keep it for as long as we actually need it to serve you or follow the law.
