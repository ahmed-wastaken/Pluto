Services

Full Service Contracting

As a highway, electrical utility contractor, Tri-State Electric has over 50 years experience installing high quality electrical work from 64 kV high and medium voltage systems down to low voltage, telecom, security and fire alarm systems.

Insured & Bonded

Government Subcontracting

Utility and Power Lines

Telecom and Security
