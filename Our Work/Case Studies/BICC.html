The Problem

Fragmented Communication, Manual Report Generation, and Escalating Licensing Fees

British International College of Cairo needed an efficient way to connect teachers, students, and guardians. Prior to the project:

  • Operational and academic communication between school staff and families lacked a unified, direct channel.
  • Staff faced heavy administrative burdens manually tracking attendance, compiling grades, and assembling weekly and end-of-year academic reports.
  • The school was exposed to costly, recurring third-party software subscriptions that drained budget year after year.
The Solution

A Streamlined Custom LMS Built on Google for Education

To eliminate administrative friction and remove third-party vendor lock-in, a custom LMS was engineered directly on the Google for Education platform:

Direct Guardian Communication & Alerts

The platform automates student performance notifications, attendance logs, and grade tracking, delivering updates directly to guardians without teacher intervention.

Automated Academic Reporting

Built-in reporting engines automatically compile and format both weekly progress summaries and comprehensive end-of-year academic report cards.

Cost-Efficient Infrastructure

Leveraging the Google for Education environment replaced expensive external SaaS licenses, drastically lowering operational costs from year one.
