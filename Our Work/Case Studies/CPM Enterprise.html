The Problem

Dry Spells, Burnt-Out Sales Reps, and Damaged Deliverability

Before working with us, CPM Enterprise was stuck in the classic outbound trap.

Their sales team was spending their days manually hunting down prospects, scraping emails, and sending out cold messages one by one. Because they were doing this without proper technical setup (no dedicated domains, warmups, or SPF/DKIM/DMARC protocols), their domain reputation took a massive hit and emails were landing straight in spam.

Even worse, every hour their team spent searching for leads was an hour they weren't closing deals. Whenever they got busy delivering work, prospecting stopped completely, leading to constant dry spells and inconsistent cash flow.

They didn't need another expensive retainer agency to drop vague reports on their desk; they needed a plug-and-play outbound engine that handed their sales team qualified meetings and let them pay only for results.

The Solution

A Hands-Off, Pay-Per-Lead Outbound Engine

We stepped in to completely remove the technical and operational burden of cold outreach from their plate.

Bulletproof Domain Infrastructure

We built a dedicated secondary domain infrastructure from scratch, dialed in SPF, DKIM, and DMARC protocols, and handled the warmups to guarantee inbox placement while keeping CPM's main business domain 100% safe.

Laser-Targeted Lists

We stopped the spray-and-pray approach and mapped out double-verified lead lists across the entire US:

  • The Companies: General Contractors actively running or awarded public projects between $1M and $150M.
  • The Decision-Makers: Project Managers, Estimators, and higher-ranking construction executives.

Messaging That Addressed Real Operational Headaches

Our copy hit the exact pain points keeping GC leadership up at night:

  • Running out of in-house BIM bandwidth on complex projects.
  • The risk of heavy liquidated damages if schedules slip.
  • Mitigating supply chain chaos with advanced CPM scheduling.

BIM coordination and modeling became the primary hook giving GCs an immediate, high-value solution they could plug into their awarded projects right away.

Strict Qualification (Pay-Per-Lead Model)

CPM didn't pay for vanity metrics, opens, or bad fits. Every prospect that landed on their calendar had to meet strict criteria before it counted:

  • Verified project awarded at $1M+.
  • Verified decision-maker title (PM, Estimator, or higher).
  • Real, active interest in BIM or scheduling support.
