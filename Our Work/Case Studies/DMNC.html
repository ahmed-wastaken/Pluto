The Problem

Fragmented Spreadsheets, Disconnected Installments, and Multi-Currency Friction

As an industrial CNC machinery manufacturer, Beijing Dimon CNC Technology Co., Ltd. manages high-ticket machine sales involving structured installment agreements, staggered balance settlements, and international client accounts.

Prior to implementing a dedicated platform, the company relied on a patchwork of disconnected spreadsheets (Excel and Google Sheets) and commercial SaaS tools:

Spreadsheet-Driven Balances

Tracking active installment plans, balance reductions, and customer balances across multiple Excel and Google Sheets created fragmented data silos and manual bookkeeping friction.

Multi-Currency Transactions

The business conducts operations across four distinct currencies US Dollars (USD), Egyptian Pounds (EGP), Chinese Yuan (CNY), and UAE Dirhams (AED). Managing cross-border balances and billing schedules across separate sheets introduced calculation complexities and delayed ledger visibility.

Manual Payment Reminders

Without automated installment triggers, staff had to manually monitor due dates across spreadsheets to send payment notices, risking delayed collections

Mounting SaaS Costs

Relying on multiple off-the-shelf software tools created compounding recurring subscription fees without delivering a single unified system that fit their installment operations.

The Solution

A Purpose-Built CRM and Automated Multi-Currency Billing Platform

To replace fragmented spreadsheets and off-the-shelf SaaS subscriptions, we architected and deployed a tailored, single-asset CRM and billing engine built directly around Dimon CNC's commercial workflow.

Centralized Client & Balance Management

Replaced standalone Excel and Google Sheets with an integrated platform that consolidates client records, machine orders, installment milestones, and real-time outstanding balances in one place.

Automated Email Reminders & Invoicing

integrated automated billing workflows that generate itemized invoices and dispatch scheduled monthly installment payment reminders directly to clients via email.

Multi-Currency Architecture

Built-in native support for four primary operating currencies, USD, EGP, CNY, and AED allowing the team to invoice, record incoming installments, and manage account balances across borders without third-party workarounds.

Complete Ownership Without Subscriptions

Deployed as an outright business asset with zero per-user seat fees or recurring platform subscriptions.
