Frequently Asked Questions

Who actually owns the software once it is built?

You do. You own 100% of the software, the source code, the designs, and the database. Unlike typical software providers who rent access month-to-month, we hand over everything when the project wraps up. You never owe us royalties or recurring fees to keep using it.

How does paying once compare to monthly subscriptions?

Most companies pay steep monthly per-user fees across 4 or 5 different software tools. As you hire more people, those monthly bills keep multiplying. With custom software, you pay once to build the exact system you need. Whether you have 5 staff members or 500, your software cost stays flat at zero ongoing seat fees.

Can you move our existing files and spreadsheets into the new system?

Yes. Moving your data is part of every project. We take your client spreadsheets, old project histories, and contact books, clean them up, and import them right into your new platform so you can get to work without missing a beat.

Where does the software live, and who controls the server?

The software is installed on your own private cloud account (like Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure) or on your company's own office servers. You have the master keys and complete control over your private records.

What if we need new features in a year or two?

Because the code is clean, modern, and completely yours, it can be updated anytime. You can hire us to build new modules as your company grows, or hand the code over to any software developer or in-house IT team to make updates using our clear documentation.

How do you make sure our financial records and company data stay safe?

Security is built into every layer. We set up role-based access so team members only see what they need, two-factor authentication for logins, and banking-grade encryption for all stored files and transactions.

Do you offer support and help after the software goes live?

Yes. Every platform comes with a dedicated warranty and stabilization period where we ensure everything runs smoothly. After launch, we offer optional support plans to keep your servers updated, backed up, and running quickly.
