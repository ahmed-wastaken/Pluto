11.9 Web Design Standards and Usability Guidelines

This comprehensive section outlines the principles, best practices, and standards that must be followed when designing and developing websites unless clearly stated otherwise in the project proposal.

User-Centered Design

  • User Research & Personas: Before embarking on any web design project, conduct thorough user research to understand the target audience. Create user personas representing different segments of the audience to create a personalized and relevant user experience.
  • Usability Testing & Evaluation: Involve real users to evaluate the design's usability iteratively. Ensure only useful content is present, as unnecessary content may hinder performance. Define goals in terms of usability and set methods to reach those goals.
  • Optimizing the User Experience: Avoid pop-ups or unsolicited windows. Keep a consistent sequence of processes to familiarize users with steps. Indicate wait times if they exceed a minute or two. Make important pages easy to print, include obvious FAQs, avoid confusing terminology, and support new or inexperienced users.
  • Typography, Layout & Visuals: Choose appropriate fonts for the audience that align with brand identity and ensure they are available across devices. Use hierarchical heading tags (H1, H2, etc.) properly. Maintain typography consistency throughout the website. Establish a clear visual hierarchy and whitespace effectively to improve readability. Use a well-defined color palette and incorporate visual elements strategically with a cohesive style.
  • Responsive Design & Imagery: Design websites to adapt seamlessly to various devices and screen sizes. Implement responsive images to serve appropriately sized assets based on the user's device.
  • Interaction & Forms: Employ subtle hover effects and feedback mechanisms. Use transitions and animations judiciously. Streamline form design by breaking them into manageable steps with clear labels, and implement input validation. Include loading and progress indicators. Follow established design patterns and maintain consistency in Ul elements and across different devices. Create design guidelines to ensure consistency among team members.

Accessibility

  • Compliance & Inclusivity: Comply with WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). Cater to diverse audiences by providing alternative text for images, offering keyboard navigation, and ensuring proper color contrast.
  • Typography & Color for Accessibility: Select accessible fonts and ensure sufficient contrast between text and background colors. Verify that text remains legible against backgrounds and use tools to check color contrast ratios. Regularly test for accessibility using automated tools and manual methods.

Brand Consistency & Navigation

  • Adhere to the client's brand guidelines (color schemes, typography, logos) to foster recognition.
  • Design intuitive menus, establish a clear content hierarchy, and implement breadcrumbs and wayfinding elements to help users understand their location within the site.

Typography, Readability & Content Strategy

  • Pay attention to font size, line spacing, and contrast to optimize legibility.
  • Create clear, concise content relevant to the user's needs, avoiding jargon. Organize content logically using headings and bullet points. Incorporate optimized visuals to enhance content.

Cross-Browser Compatibility

  • Test websites on a range of browsers and versions. Implement graceful degradation for older browsers and adopt a progressive enhancement approach.
